## 1. Introduction to AI-Driven Precision Phytopathology
Plant diseases destroy an estimated 20% to 40% of global crop production annually, resulting in direct economic losses exceeding $220 billion.
CropVision bridges the gap between laboratory phytopathology and resource-constrained smallholder farmers by delivering real-time neural leaf pathology analysis on standard commodity smartphones.

### Key Principles of Optical Leaf Diagnosis
- **Chromatic Separation**: Isolating healthy photosynthetic leaf tissue (chlorophyll a/b reflectance peak at 550nm) from necrotic or chlorotic fungal haustoria lesions.
- **Morphological Pattern Recognition**: Distinguishing between angular vein-delimited bacterial spots (e.g. *Xanthomonas*) and circular concentric fungal lesions (e.g. *Alternaria solani*).
- **Environmental Cross-Referencing**: Fusing optical model predictions with hourly microclimate parameters (Mills infection periods, relative humidity thresholds) to calibrate diagnostic confidence.

## 7. Crop-by-Crop Diagnostic Protocols
### 7.1 Pathological Profile & Treatment Protocol #1
#### Overview & Causal Agent
Profile #1 examines pathogen lifecycle, spore dissemination aerodynamics, and host susceptibility across phenological stages.

#### Symptom Progression Matrix
| Growth Stage | Visual Symptoms | Target Tissue | Severity Index | Action Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seedling (BBCH 10-19) | Damping off, hypocotyl water-soaked necrosis | Basal stem | Low to Moderate | Immediate seed treatment |
| Vegetative (BBCH 20-39) | Expanding concentric circular lesions, chlorotic halo | Lower canopy foliage | Moderate | Preventative contact spray |
| Flowering (BBCH 50-69) | Blossom blight, pedicel infection, floral drop | Reproductive organs | Critical | Systemic curative spray |
| Fruit Set (BBCH 70-89) | Sunken leathery dark lesions, premature fruit drop | Fruit epidermis | Severe | Salvage harvest & sanitation |

#### Integrated Treatment Strategy
1. **Cultural Sanitation**: Remove bottom 20cm of foliage touching soil. Sterilize shears with 70% isopropyl alcohol.
2. **Biological Stewardship**: Apply *Bacillus subtilis* QST 713 at 2.5 kg/ha or *Trichoderma harzianum* T-22 at transplanting.
3. **Chemical Alternation**: Rotate between FRAC Group 11 (Strobilurins) and FRAC Group 3 (Triazoles) mixed with multi-site Mancozeb.
4. **Irrigation Protocol**: Shift from overhead sprinkler to drip irrigation to keep leaf wetness duration under 4 hours.
