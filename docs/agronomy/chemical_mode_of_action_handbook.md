## Executive Summary
Repeated mono-therapeutic application of single-site systemic fungicides induces severe directional selection,
leading to field-evolved target-site mutations (e.g. G143A in cyt b gene conferring strobilurin resistance).
This handbook provides the authoritative biochemical classification and rotation guidelines for CropVision.

---

## FRAC Group 1: Methyl Benzimidazole Carbamates (MBC)
- **Primary Biochemical Target**: Tubulin polymerization inhibition in beta-tubulin gene
- **Key Commercial Active Ingredients**: Carbendazim, Thiophanate-Methyl
- **Resistance Development Potential**: High Risk

### Stewardship & Rotation Protocol
1. Maximum of 2 consecutive sprays of FRAC Group 1 before alternating to an unrelated mode of action.
2. Always tank-mix single-site systemic fungicides with a multi-site protectant (e.g. Mancozeb or Copper).
3. Adhere strictly to the manufacturer Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) to guarantee zero residue violations.
